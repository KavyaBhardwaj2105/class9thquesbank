Section A — Multiple Choice Questions (15)

Q1. In an ordered pair (−4, 7), which number represents the horizontal position?

Correct Answer: −4. The first coordinate gives the horizontal position.

Q2. A point is 5 units to the right of the origin and 3 units below it. What are its coordinates?

Correct Answer: (5, −3). Right means positive x and below means negative y.

Q3. Which point lies on the vertical axis?

Correct Answer: (0, −8). Every point on the vertical axis has x-coordinate 0.

Q4. Which point is closest to the origin among the following?

Correct Answer: (1, −2). Its distance from the origin is √5, smaller than the distances of the other points.

Q5. If a point moves from (−2, 4) to (5, 4), how far has it moved horizontally?

Correct Answer: 7 units. The y-coordinate stays 4, while x changes from −2 to 5: 5 − (−2) = 7.

Q6. Which point has the same vertical position as (−7, 6)?

Correct Answer: (9, 6). Points with the same y-coordinate lie on the same horizontal level.

Q7. A point has coordinates (−9, −2). In which direction is it from the origin?

Correct Answer: Left and below. Both coordinates are negative.

Q8. The point (0, 12) is located:

Correct Answer: 12 units above the origin.

Q9. Which point is a reflection of (4, −7) across the horizontal axis?

Correct Answer: (4, 7). Reflection across the horizontal axis changes the sign of y only.

Q10. If A = (−3, 5) and B = (−3, −4), what can be concluded?

Correct Answer: Same vertical line. Their x-coordinate is −3 in both cases.

Q11. A point is moved 6 units left from (8, −1). What is its new position?

Correct Answer: (2, −1). Moving left decreases the x-coordinate by 6.

Q12. Which pair represents two points that are equally far from the vertical axis?

Correct Answer: (−6, 4) and (6, −9). Their horizontal distances from the vertical axis are both 6 units.

Q13. A rectangular garden has corners P(2,1), Q(8,1), R(8,5). What are the coordinates of the fourth corner?

Correct Answer: (2,5). The fourth corner must have x = 2 and y = 5.

Q14. A point (a, −6) lies 4 units to the left of the y-axis. Find a.

Correct Answer: −4. Four units left of the vertical axis means x = −4.

Q15. Which statement about the point (−5, 0) is correct?

Correct Answer: 5 units left of the origin.

Section B — True / False (10)

Q16. The origin is represented by the ordered pair (0,0).

Correct Answer: True.

Q17. In an ordered pair, the second coordinate always describes horizontal movement.

Correct Answer: False. The first coordinate represents horizontal position and the second represents vertical position.

Q18. Every point whose x-coordinate is zero lies on the vertical axis.

Correct Answer: True.

Q19. The points (−4,3) and (4,3) have the same vertical position.

Correct Answer: True. Both have y = 3.

Q20. Moving upward by 5 units changes only the x-coordinate.

Correct Answer: False. Vertical movement changes the y-coordinate.

Q21. The points (7,−2) and (7,8) lie on the same vertical line.

Correct Answer: True. Their x-coordinate is the same.

Q22. A point with both coordinates negative lies to the left and below the origin.

Correct Answer: True.

Q23. The points (−8,0) and (0,−8) represent the same location.

Correct Answer: False. They lie on different axes and represent different positions.

Q24. Reflecting a point across the vertical axis changes the sign of its x-coordinate.

Correct Answer: True.

Q25. The point (−3,4) is farther from the y-axis than the point (5,−1).

Correct Answer: False. Distances from the y-axis are 3 and 5 units respectively.

Q26. Assertion (A): The point (0,−9) lies on the vertical axis.
Reason (R): Every point on the vertical axis has x-coordinate zero.

Correct Answer: A.

Q27. Assertion (A): The points (3,5) and (3,−2) lie on the same vertical line.
Reason (R): Points having equal x-coordinates have the same horizontal position.

Correct Answer: A. Equal x-coordinates place points on the same vertical line.

Q28. Assertion (A): The point (−6,−4) lies to the right of the origin.
Reason (R): A negative x-coordinate indicates a position to the left of the vertical axis.

Correct Answer: D. The assertion is false, while the reason is true.

Q29. Assertion (A): The points (−2,7) and (5,7) are on the same horizontal line.
Reason (R): Their y-coordinates are equal.

Correct Answer: A.

Q30. Assertion (A): Reflecting (−5,3) across the horizontal axis gives (5,−3).
Reason (R): Reflection across the horizontal axis changes the sign of the y-coordinate only.

Correct Answer: D. The correct reflection is (−5,−3). The reason is true.

Section D — Fill in the Blanks (5)

Q31. The point where the horizontal and vertical axes meet is called the ______.

Correct Answer: Origin.

Q32. The ordered pair (0,−11) lies on the ______ axis.

Correct Answer: Vertical.

Q33. Moving 4 units downward changes the y-coordinate by ______.

Correct Answer: −4.

Q34. The distance of the point (−9,4) from the vertical axis is ______ units.

Correct Answer: 9. Distance from the vertical axis depends on the absolute value of x.

Q35. In the coordinate pair (−8,13), the number 13 is the ______ coordinate.

Correct Answer: Second.

Section E — Match the Following (5)

Q36. Match each point with its location relative to the origin.

(a) (4,6)
(b) (−3,8)
(c) (7,−5)
(d) (−2,−9)
Correct Match: (a) right & above, (b) left & above, (c) right & below, (d) left & below.

Q37. Match each coordinate condition with the correct description.

(a) x = 0
(b) y = 0
(c) x > 0
(d) y < 0
Correct Match: x=0 → vertical axis; y=0 → horizontal axis; x>0 → right; y<0 → below.

Q38. Match each movement with its effect on coordinates.

(a) Move 5 units right
(b) Move 3 units left
(c) Move 7 units up
(d) Move 2 units down
Correct Match: right → x+5; left → x−3; up → y+7; down → y−2.

Q39. Match the pair of points with their relationship.

(a) (−4,2) and (6,2)
(b) (5,−3) and (5,8)
(c) (0,−6) and (0,4)
(d) (−7,0) and (3,0)
Correct Match: (a) horizontal, (b) vertical, (c) vertical axis, (d) horizontal axis.

Q40. Match each point with its distance from the indicated axis.

(a) (−8,5) from vertical axis
(b) (4,−9) from horizontal axis
(c) (−3,7) from vertical axis
(d) (6,−2) from horizontal axis
Correct Match: (a) 8 units, (b) 9 units, (c) 3 units, (d) 2 units.

Section F — Drag & Drop Style Questions (5)

Q41. Place/select the coordinate reached by starting at (−6,2) and moving 9 units right.

Correct Answer: (3,2). The x-coordinate increases from −6 to 3.

Q42. Select the coordinate obtained by moving 6 units downward from (−1,5).

Correct Answer: (−1,−1). Downward movement decreases y by 6.

Q43. Select the point that is the reflection of (−7,4) in the vertical axis.

Correct Answer: (7,4). Reflection in the vertical axis changes x only.

Q44. A point starts at (2,−3). It moves 4 units left and then 8 units up. Select its final position.

Correct Answer: (−2,5). First x: 2−4=−2. Then y: −3+8=5.

Q45. Which point can be reached from (−3,−5) by moving 10 units right and 2 units down?

Correct Answer: (7,−7). x = −3+10 = 7 and y = −5−2 = −7.

Section G — HOTS / Application Based Questions (10)

Q46. A robot starts at (−4,3). It moves 7 units right, 5 units down, and then 2 units left. Where does it stop?

Correct Answer: (1,−2). x: −4+7−2=1; y: 3−5=−2.

Q47. Points A(−2,6), B(5,6), C(5,−1) are three vertices of a rectangle. Find the fourth vertex D.

Correct Answer: (−2,−1). D must have x = −2 like A and y = −1 like C.

Q48. A treasure map marks the school at (−3,4) and the library at (6,4). A student walks directly along the same horizontal path. How many units does the student travel?

Correct Answer: 9 units. The y-coordinate is unchanged, so distance = 6−(−3)=9.

Q49. A point P is equally far from both axes and lies in the region left of the vertical axis and above the horizontal axis. Which could be P?

Correct Answer: (−6,6). It is left, above, and its distances from both axes are 6 units.

Q50. A point starts at (8,−2). After an unknown horizontal movement, it becomes (−5,−2). How many units and in which direction did it move?

Correct Answer: 13 units left. The horizontal change is −5−8=−13.

Q51. A rectangular playground has opposite corners A(−6,−4) and C(3,5), with sides parallel to the axes. What is the length of its horizontal side?

Correct Answer: 9 units. Horizontal length = 3−(−6)=9.

Q52. A point X is 5 units from the vertical axis and 8 units below the horizontal axis. It is also to the left of the vertical axis. What are its coordinates?

Correct Answer: (−5,−8). Left gives x=−5 and below gives y=−8.

Q53. A point A(−2,−3) is reflected first across the vertical axis and then across the horizontal axis. Where does it finally lie?

Correct Answer: (2,3). Vertical reflection changes x: (2,−3). Horizontal reflection changes y: (2,3).

Q54. A delivery route goes from A(−5,2) to B(−5,−6) and then to C(4,−6). What is the total distance travelled if movement follows the axes?

Correct Answer: 17 units. A→B = 8 units and B→C = 9 units. Total = 8+9 = 17 units.

Q55. Four points are given: A(−4,3), B(2,3), C(2,−5), D(−4,−5). Which statement best describes the figure formed?

Correct Answer: A rectangle whose sides are parallel to the coordinate axes. Horizontal sides have equal y-values and vertical sides have equal x-values. Its dimensions are 6 × 8, so it is not a square.
