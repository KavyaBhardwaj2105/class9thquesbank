Section A — Multiple Choice Questions

Q1. A lift rises from the 4th floor to the 11th floor. How many floors does it move upward?

✓ Correct Answer: 7. 11 − 4 = 7.

Q2. The radius of a circular plate is 8 cm. What is its diameter?

✓ Correct Answer: 16 cm. Diameter = 2 × radius.

Q3. A child walks 9 m north and then 9 m south. What is the final displacement?

✓ Correct Answer: 0 m. The child returns to the starting point.

Q4. What angle represents one-fourth of a complete revolution?

✓ Correct Answer: 90°.

Q5. A wheel completes 4 complete rotations. What is the total angle turned?

✓ Correct Answer: 1440°. 4 × 360° = 1440°.

Q6. Which angle represents a half turn?

✓ Correct Answer: 180°.

Q7. A point moves 15 units right and then 6 units left. What is its net movement?

✓ Correct Answer: 9 units right.

Q8. A circle has diameter 26 cm. What is its radius?

✓ Correct Answer: 13 cm.

Q9. A rotating pointer turns through 270°. What fraction of a full turn is this?

✓ Correct Answer: 3/4.

Q10. A student walks 5 m east and then 12 m north. What is the straight-line distance from the starting point?

✓ Correct Answer: 13 m.

Q11. A point moves 18 units downward and then 25 units upward. What is the net movement?

✓ Correct Answer: 7 units upward.

Q12. How many degrees are there in two-and-a-half complete turns?

✓ Correct Answer: 900°.

Q13. Which statement is always true for a circle?

✓ Correct Answer: Diameter is twice the radius.

Q14. A pointer turns 135°. What part of a full revolution has it completed?

✓ Correct Answer: 3/8.

Q15. A robot moves 7 steps left, 4 steps right and then 10 steps left. What is its final position relative to the start?

✓ Correct Answer: 13 steps left.

Section B — True / False

Q16. A complete revolution measures 360°.

✓ Correct Answer: True.

Q17. A quarter turn is equal to 180°.

✓ Correct Answer: False. It is 90°.

Q18. The diameter passes through the centre of a circle.

✓ Correct Answer: True.

Q19. A full turn and two half turns represent the same amount of rotation.

✓ Correct Answer: True.

Q20. If an object returns to its starting point, its displacement is zero.

✓ Correct Answer: True.

Q21. The radius of a circle is always longer than its diameter.

✓ Correct Answer: False.

Q22. Three half turns make one and a half complete turns.

✓ Correct Answer: True.

Q23. Moving 6 units east and 2 units west results in 8 units east.

✓ Correct Answer: False. Net movement is 4 units east.

Q24. A three-quarter turn measures 270°.

✓ Correct Answer: True.

Q25. The diameter of a circle with radius 11 cm is 22 cm.

✓ Correct Answer: True.

Section C — Assertion & Reasoning

A = Both A and R are true and R explains A
B = Both A and R are true but R does not explain A
C = A is true but R is false
D = A is false but R is true

Q26. Assertion: A complete revolution is 360°.
Reason: A complete revolution brings an object back to its original orientation.

✓ Correct Answer: B. Both are true, but the reason does not explain the numerical measure 360°.

Q27. Assertion: A circle with radius 17 cm has diameter 34 cm.
Reason: Diameter is twice the radius.

✓ Correct Answer: A.

Q28. Assertion: A quarter turn is 180°.
Reason: A half turn is 180°.

✓ Correct Answer: D. The assertion is false; a quarter turn is 90°. The reason is true.

Q29. Assertion: A person travelling 12 m north and then 12 m south has zero displacement.
Reason: The person's final position is the same as the initial position.

✓ Correct Answer: A.

Q30. Assertion: Five quarter turns make 450°.
Reason: One quarter turn is 90°.

✓ Correct Answer: A.

Section G — HOTS / Higher Order Thinking

Q46. A robot starts at 2 on a number line. It moves 9 units right, 13 units left and then 6 units right. Where does it finish?

✓ Correct Answer: 4.

Q47. A rotating disk turns 120° clockwise and then 150° anticlockwise. What is its net rotation?

✓ Correct Answer: 30° anticlockwise.

Q48. A circular table has diameter 36 cm. A straight line joining two opposite points through the centre is drawn. What is its length?

✓ Correct Answer: 36 cm. The line is the diameter.

Q49. A hiker walks 8 km east, 15 km north and then 8 km west. How far north of the starting point is the hiker?

✓ Correct Answer: 15 km north.

Q50. A machine dial has completed 5/8 of a turn. How many degrees remain before one complete turn?

✓ Correct Answer: 135°.

Q51. Two circles have radii 9 cm and 16 cm. What is the difference between their diameters?

✓ Correct Answer: 14 cm.

Q52. A pointer starts facing east. It makes a 270° anticlockwise turn. Which direction does it finally face?

✓ Correct Answer: South.

Q53. A lift begins at floor 6, goes down 9 floors, then rises 12 floors and finally goes down 4 floors. Which floor does it reach?

✓ Correct Answer: 5th floor.

Q54. A wheel rotates 1¾ turns. Through what total angle has it rotated?

✓ Correct Answer: 630°.

Q55. A student walks 7 m north, 24 m east, 7 m south and 24 m west. What is the shortest distance between the student's final and initial positions?

✓ Correct Answer: 0 m.
