SECTION A — Multiple Choice Questions (15)

Q1. Which of the following is the greatest number?

Correct Answer: 8,01,245.

Q2. What is the place value of 6 in 46,32,781?

Correct Answer: 6,00,000. The digit 6 is in the lakh place.

Q3. Which is the correct expanded form of 5,07,304?

Correct Answer: 5,00,000 + 7,000 + 300 + 4.

Q4. Which number is represented by 9 × 10,000 + 4 × 1,000 + 6 × 10 + 2?

Correct Answer: 94,062.

Q5. What is the smallest 6-digit number?

Correct Answer: 1,00,000.

Q6. Which number comes immediately after 99,999?

Correct Answer: 1,00,000.

Q7. Which number is divisible by 5?

Correct Answer: 76,315. Its last digit is 5.

Q8. Which of these numbers is divisible by 3?

Correct Answer: 5,436. Sum of digits = 18, which is divisible by 3.

Q9. What is the remainder when 47 is divided by 6?

Correct Answer: 5. 47 = 6 × 7 + 5.

Q10. Which number is a common multiple of 4 and 6?

Correct Answer: 24.

Q11. Which number is prime?

Correct Answer: 29. It has exactly two factors: 1 and 29.

Q12. Which pair contains only composite numbers?

Correct Answer: 18 and 27.

Q13. What is the greatest 4-digit number divisible by 10?

Correct Answer: 9,990.

Q14. Which statement about zero is correct?

Correct Answer: Zero is neither prime nor composite.

Q15. If a number is divisible by both 2 and 3, it must also be divisible by:

Correct Answer: 6.

SECTION B — True / False (10)

Q16. Every multiple of 10 ends in zero.

Correct Answer: True.

Q17. 1 is a prime number.

Correct Answer: False. A prime number has exactly two factors, while 1 has only one.

Q18. Every even number is divisible by 2.

Correct Answer: True.

Q19. 3,456 is divisible by 9 because the sum of its digits is 18.

Correct Answer: True.

Q20. A number ending in 5 is always divisible by 10.

Correct Answer: False. Such a number is divisible by 5, not necessarily by 10.

Q21. The smallest prime number is 2.

Correct Answer: True.

Q22. Every composite number has more than two factors.

Correct Answer: True.

Q23. 84 is divisible by both 4 and 7.

Correct Answer: True. 84 ÷ 4 = 21 and 84 ÷ 7 = 12.

Q24. The number 72 has exactly four factors.

Correct Answer: False. 72 has more than four factors.

Q25. If a number is divisible by 6, it must be divisible by both 2 and 3.

Correct Answer: True.

SECTION C — Assertion & Reasoning (5)

Q26. Assertion (A): 4,725 is divisible by 5.
Reason (R): A number ending in 0 or 5 is divisible by 5.

Correct Answer: A.

Q27. Assertion (A): 48 is divisible by 6.
Reason (R): 48 is divisible by both 2 and 3.

Correct Answer: A. Divisibility by both 2 and 3 gives divisibility by 6.

Q28. Assertion (A): 1 is neither prime nor composite.
Reason (R): 1 has exactly one factor.

Correct Answer: A.

Q29. Assertion (A): 3,240 is divisible by 10.
Reason (R): Its last digit is 4.

Q30. Assertion (A): 37 is a prime number.
Reason (R): 37 has no factor other than 1 and 37.

Correct Answer: A.

SECTION G — HOTS / Higher Order Thinking (10)

Q46. Using the digits 2, 5, 7 and 9 exactly once, form the greatest possible 4-digit number.

Correct Answer: 9,752. Arrange the digits from greatest to smallest.

Q47. A school has 3,875 students in one year and 4,126 students the next year. By how many students did the number increase?

Correct Answer: 251. 4,126 − 3,875 = 251.

Q48. Find the smallest number greater than 100 that is divisible by both 8 and 12.

Correct Answer: 120. It is the first common multiple of 8 and 12 greater than 100.

Q49. A number is divisible by 2, 5 and 3. Which is the smallest possible positive number satisfying all three conditions?

Correct Answer: 30. It is divisible by 2, 3 and 5.

Q50. A four-digit number has 7 in the thousands place, 0 in the hundreds place, 4 in the tens place and 9 in the ones place. What is the number?

Correct Answer: 7,049.

Q51. Ravi says that 91 is prime because it is odd. Is his reasoning correct?

Correct Answer: No, 91 = 7 × 13. Oddness alone does not make a number prime.

Q52. A number has digits 4, 6 and 8. Each digit is used once. Which arrangement gives the greatest number divisible by 2?

Correct Answer: 864. It is the greatest arrangement and ends in an even digit.

Q53. Three bells ring every 6, 8 and 12 minutes. If they ring together at 9:00 a.m., after how many minutes will they ring together again?

Correct Answer: 24 minutes. 24 is the least common multiple of 6, 8 and 12.

Q54. A number leaves remainder 3 when divided by 5. Which of the following could be that number?

Correct Answer: 48. 48 = 5 × 9 + 3.

Q55. Find the smallest 5-digit number that is divisible by both 5 and 9.

Correct Answer: 10,035. It ends in 5 and its digit sum is 9, so it is divisible by both 5 and 9.
