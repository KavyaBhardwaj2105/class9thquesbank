Topics: Perimeter, Area, rectangles, squares, triangles, composite figures, missing dimensions and real-life measurement problems.

Section A — Multiple Choice Questions

Q1. A rectangle has length 18 cm and breadth 7 cm. What is its perimeter?

✓ Correct Answer: 50 cm. Perimeter = 2(l + b) = 2(18 + 7) = 50 cm.

Q2. What is the area of a square whose side is 13 cm?

✓ Correct Answer: 169 cm². Area of square = side × side = 13 × 13.

Q3. A rectangular garden is 24 m long and 15 m wide. How much ground does it cover?

✓ Correct Answer: 360 m². Area = 24 × 15 = 360 m².

Q4. The perimeter of a square is 64 cm. What is the length of each side?

✓ Correct Answer: 16 cm. Side = perimeter ÷ 4 = 64 ÷ 4.

Q5. Which unit is most suitable for measuring the area of a classroom floor?

✓ Correct Answer: m². Area of a floor is measured in square units.

Q6. A rectangle has area 144 cm² and length 12 cm. Find its breadth.

✓ Correct Answer: 12 cm. Breadth = Area ÷ length = 144 ÷ 12.

Q7. A square and a rectangle both have perimeter 40 cm. The rectangle has length 14 cm. What is its breadth?

✓ Correct Answer: 6 cm. 2(14 + b) = 40, so 14 + b = 20 and b = 6 cm.

Q8. What is the area of a rectangle measuring 9 cm by 16 cm?

✓ Correct Answer: 144 cm². Area = 9 × 16.

Q9. A rectangular photo frame has length 20 cm and breadth 12 cm. How much border length is needed to go around it once?

✓ Correct Answer: 64 cm. Border length means perimeter = 2(20 + 12).

Q10. A square tile has area 225 cm². What is the length of one side?

✓ Correct Answer: 15 cm because 15 × 15 = 225.

Q11. A rectangle has length 15 cm and breadth 6 cm. What is its area?

✓ Correct Answer: 90 cm². Area = length × breadth = 15 × 6 = 90 cm².

Q12. If the length and breadth of a rectangle are both doubled, its area becomes:

✓ Correct Answer: 4 times. New area = (2l)(2b) = 4lb.

Q13. A rectangular field is 35 m long and 20 m wide. What distance will a person cover by walking once around it?

✓ Correct Answer: 110 m. Perimeter = 2(35 + 20) = 110 m.

Q14. A square has side 9 cm. If its side is increased to 12 cm, by how much does its area increase?

✓ Correct Answer: 63 cm². Original area = 81 cm²; new area = 144 cm²; increase = 63 cm².

Q15. Which rectangle has the greatest area if all three have perimeter 24 cm?

✓ Correct Answer: 6 cm × 6 cm. Their areas are 20, 32, 36 and 27 cm² respectively.

Section B — True / False

Q16. Perimeter measures the length around a closed figure.

✓ Correct Answer: True.

Q17. Area is measured in ordinary units such as centimetres rather than square units.

✓ Correct Answer: False. Area is measured in square units such as cm² and m².

Q18. Two rectangles can have the same area but different perimeters.

✓ Correct Answer: True. For example, 4 × 12 and 6 × 8 both have area 48 but different perimeters.

Q19. The area of a square of side 8 cm is 32 cm².

✓ Correct Answer: False. Area = 8 × 8 = 64 cm².

Q20. If the side of a square is tripled, its perimeter becomes three times as large.

✓ Correct Answer: True. Perimeter changes directly with the side length.

Q21. A rectangle of length 10 cm and breadth 5 cm has perimeter 30 cm.

✓ Correct Answer: True. 2(10 + 5) = 30 cm.

Q22. A triangle with base 12 cm and height 8 cm has area 96 cm².

✓ Correct Answer: False. Area = ½ × 12 × 8 = 48 cm².

Q23. Increasing only the length of a rectangle increases its area if its breadth remains fixed.

✓ Correct Answer: True.

Q24. A square with side 10 m has area 100 m².

✓ Correct Answer: True.

Q25. If two figures have equal perimeters, they must always have equal areas.

✓ Correct Answer: False. Figures can have the same perimeter but different areas.

Section C — Assertion & Reasoning

A = Both A and R are true and R explains A
B = Both A and R are true but R does not explain A
C = A is true but R is false
D = A is false but R is true

Q26. Assertion: A rectangle of length 12 cm and breadth 5 cm has area 60 cm².
Reason: Area of a rectangle is length × breadth.

✓ Correct Answer: A. Both statements are true and the reason explains the assertion.

Q27. Assertion: The area of a rectangle measuring 12 cm × 5 cm is 60 cm².
Reason: Area of a rectangle is length × breadth.

✓ Correct Answer: B. Both Assertion and Reason are true, but the reason directly explains the calculation of the area, so actually the correct A/R classification should be A.

Q28. Assertion: A triangle with base 20 cm and height 6 cm has area 60 cm².
Reason: The area of a triangle is half the product of its base and corresponding height.

✓ Correct Answer: A. ½ × 20 × 6 = 60 cm².

Q29. Assertion: A rectangle measuring 15 cm × 4 cm has the same area as a square of side 7 cm.
Reason: The area of the rectangle is 60 cm².

✓ Correct Answer: D. The assertion is false because the square has area 49 cm², while the reason is true.

Q30. Assertion: If the side of a square is doubled, its area becomes four times.
Reason: Area depends on the square of the side length.

✓ Correct Answer: A. Original area = s²; new area = (2s)² = 4s².

Section D — Fill in the Blanks

Q31. The perimeter of a rectangle with length l and breadth b is ______.

✓ Correct Answer: 2(l + b).

Q32. The area of a square with side 11 cm is ______ cm².

✓ Correct Answer: 121 cm².

Q33. If a rectangle has area 96 cm² and breadth 8 cm, its length is ______ cm.

✓ Correct Answer: 12 cm. 96 ÷ 8 = 12.

Q34. The area of a triangle with base 14 cm and height 9 cm is ______ cm².

✓ Correct Answer: 63 cm². ½ × 14 × 9 = 63.

Q35. A square has perimeter 92 cm. Each side measures ______ cm.

✓ Correct Answer: 23 cm. 92 ÷ 4 = 23.

Section G — HOTS / Higher Order Thinking

Q46. A rectangular sheet is 30 cm long and 20 cm wide. A strip 5 cm wide is removed along the entire length. What area remains?

✓ Correct Answer: 450 cm². Original area = 30 × 20 = 600 cm². Removed strip = 30 × 5 = 150 cm². Remaining area = 600 − 150 = 450 cm².

Q47. A square playground has side 25 m. A path is marked all around its boundary. If the path follows the boundary once, what distance is covered?

✓ Correct Answer: 100 m. Boundary distance = perimeter = 4 × 25.

Q48. A rectangular field has area 480 m² and length 24 m. If fencing costs ₹15 per metre, what is the total fencing cost?

✓ Correct Answer: ₹1320. Breadth = 480 ÷ 24 = 20 m. Perimeter = 2(24 + 20) = 88 m. Cost = 88 × 15 = ₹1320.

Q49. A 12 cm × 8 cm rectangle is divided into two equal triangles by joining opposite corners. What is the area of each triangle?

✓ Correct Answer: 48 cm². Rectangle area = 96 cm², and each triangle is half.

Q50. A square and a rectangle have equal areas of 100 cm². The square has side 10 cm. If the rectangle has length 20 cm, what is its perimeter?

✓ Correct Answer: 50 cm. Rectangle breadth = 100 ÷ 20 = 5 cm. Perimeter = 2(20 + 5) = 50 cm.

Q51. A rectangular floor is 9 m by 6 m. Square tiles of side 3 m are used without cutting. How many tiles are required?

✓ Correct Answer: 6. Floor area = 54 m²; each tile = 9 m²; 54 ÷ 9 = 6.

Q52. A rectangular garden has an area of 300 cm². Which pair could be its length and breadth?

✓ Correct Answer: Length 20 cm, breadth 15 cm. 20 × 15 = 300 cm².

Q53. A rectangular floor is 35 m long and 24 m wide. What is its area?

✓ Correct Answer: 840 m². Area = 35 × 24 = 840 m².

Q54. A rectangle measures 18 cm × 10 cm. Its length is reduced by 3 cm while its breadth is increased by 3 cm. What happens to its area?

✓ Correct Answer: Area increases by 15 cm². Original area = 18 × 10 = 180 cm². New dimensions = 15 cm × 13 cm. New area = 195 cm². Increase = 195 − 180 = 15 cm².

Q55. A rectangular piece of land is 40 m × 25 m. A square portion of side 10 m is used for a shed. What percentage of the land remains unused by the shed?

✓ Correct Answer: 90%. Total area = 1000 m². Shed = 100 m². Remaining = 900 m² = 90% of total.
